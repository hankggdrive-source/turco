我們的故事
Our Story

我們是 TURCO，一家總部位於台灣、專注於跨境食品貿易及供應解決方案的公司。我們的業務涵蓋進出口兩個方向。

We are TURCO, a Taiwan-based food supplier and trading company focused on cross-border food trade and supply solutions. We connect Taiwan with global food markets through seamless two-way trade.

在進口方面，我們精心引進多款特色產品，包括土耳其咖啡、土耳其茶、水果茶、土耳其軟糖、玫瑰水、開心果醬及卡達伊夫等，每項產品均以品質與市場適配性為首要考量。

On the import side, we carefully source selected specialty products including Turkish coffee, Turkish tea, fruit teas, Turkish delight, rose water, pistachio paste, and kadayıf — each selected for its quality and strong fit with the Taiwan market.

在出口方面，身為台灣出口食品的推廣者，我們協助台灣生產商將台灣頂級茶葉、抹茶及麵條等優質產品推廣至國際合作夥伴。

On the export side, as a trusted Taiwan-based food exporter, we work closely with local producers to bring premium Taiwanese teas, matcha, and noodles to international markets.

TURCO 主要服務對象為 B2B 客戶，包括經銷商、批發商、飯店及餐廳。TURCO 的目標很簡單：讓跨境食品貿易更高效、更值得信賴、更具規模化潛力。

We operate as a B2B trading company working with distributors, wholesalers, hotels, and restaurants. At TURCO, our goal is simple: to make cross-border food trade efficient, trustworthy, and scalable.
