About Us

我們是 TURCO

We Are TURCO

我們是 TURCO，一家總部位於台灣、專注於跨境食品貿易及供應解決方案。

We are TURCO, a Taiwan-based food supplier and trading company focused on cross-border food trade and supply solutions.

我們的業務涵蓋進出口兩個方向，致力於將國際品牌與台灣市場緊密連結，同時協助台灣優質產品進軍海外市場。作為台灣食品採購與供應商，我們以可靠性與品質為核心，提供全方位的貿易支援。

We connect Taiwan with global food markets through seamless two-way trade — bringing international specialty products into Taiwan, and helping Taiwanese producers reach new markets abroad.

在進口方面，我們精心引進多款特色產品，包括土耳其咖啡、土耳其茶、水果茶、土耳其軟糖、玫瑰水、開心果醬及卡達伊夫等，每項產品均以品質與市場適配性為首要考量。

On the import side, we carefully source selected specialty products including Turkish coffee, Turkish tea, fruit teas, Turkish delight, rose water, pistachio paste, and kadayıf — each selected for its quality and strong fit with the Taiwan market.

在出口方面，身為台灣出口食品的推廣者，我們協助台灣生產商將台灣頂級茶葉、抹茶及麵條等優質產品推廣至國際合作夥伴。

On the export side, as a trusted Taiwan-based food exporter, we work closely with local producers to bring premium Taiwanese teas, matcha, and noodles to international markets.

TURCO 主要服務對象為 B2B 客戶，包括經銷商、批發商、飯店及餐廳。我們提供採購、貿易與供應解決方案，協助合作夥伴實現可持續成長。

We operate as a B2B trading company, working with distributors, wholesalers, hotels, and restaurants — providing food sourcing services in Taiwan, end-to-end trade coordination, and supply solutions that enable our partners to grow sustainably.

憑藉多年經驗與認證標準，我們專注於可靠性、一致性與長期合作。TURCO 的目標很簡單：讓跨境食品貿易更高效、更值得信賴、更具規模化潛力。

With years of experience and certified standards, we focus on reliability, consistency, and long-term collaboration. At TURCO, our goal is simple: to make cross-border food trade efficient, trustworthy, and scalable.
